# Classroom IT Runbooks

Runbooks and procedures for the team that keeps a campus's classrooms
working: the projectors, displays, audio, control systems, network,
lectern devices and sign-ins that instructors rely on at the start of
every class.

## Where to go

- **New to the team?** Begin with [Start here](start/index.md): it sets out
  your first weeks and the order to read the rest in.
- **A room has a problem?** Look the room up in [Rooms](rooms/index.md) to see
  what is installed there, then open the matching runbook in
  [Runbooks](runbooks/index.md).
- **Can't fix it, or it's out of scope?** [Procedures](procedures/index.md)
  says who to hand it to and how.
- **Writing or reviewing a page?** [Contributing](contributing/index.md) has
  the template and conventions; [Reference](reference/index.md) has the
  knowledge-base export and taxonomy.
