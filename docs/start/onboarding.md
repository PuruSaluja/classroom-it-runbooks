# Onboarding path

What a new student technician reads, shadows and does before taking calls
alone, in four stages. Each stage is planned as about a week of shifts;
move on when the shift lead agrees, not by the calendar.

## Stage 1: learn the ground

- Read this site's [home page](../index.md), then [Rooms](../rooms/index.md):
  find your home building and read three of its rooms of different types
  (a standard classroom, a hybrid classroom and a lecture hall, if it has
  them).
- Walk those rooms with the shift lead, with the room pages open, and find
  each piece of equipment the page lists.
- Read the [escalation matrix](../procedures/escalation-matrix.md) and
  [shift handoff](../procedures/shift-handoff.md). Learn the roles and the
  three urgencies; you will use them on every call that a runbook does not
  close.
- Read the safety rules in the
  [authoring conventions](../contributing/authoring-conventions.md#safety-and-remit):
  no opening equipment, no mains wiring, no work at height alone.
- Your access to the ticket queue and the AV store is requested by the
  shift lead. Never use another technician's account, and never share
  yours.

## Stage 2: shadow

- Shadow an experienced technician for your shifts this stage. Before each
  call, say which runbook you would open; after it, read that runbook and
  compare it with what was done.
- Read the first runbooks (below) in full, and the
  [Runbooks](../runbooks/index.md) index for each category.
- Pack the kit bag yourself at the start of a shift, from the list in
  [shift handoff](../procedures/shift-handoff.md#the-kit-bag).

## Stage 3: follow runbooks with a lead watching

- In an empty room, follow the Checks of each first runbook on the real
  equipment. Checks change nothing, so they are safe to practise between
  classes.
- Take calls with an experienced technician beside you: you follow the
  runbook and speak to the instructor; they step in only if needed.
- Write your part of the handoff note at the end of each shift.

## Stage 4: take calls alone

- Take calls alone, with the shift lead reachable by phone.
- Escalate through the matrix whenever a runbook's Fix does not hold;
  escalating is part of the job, not a failure.
- At the end of the stage, go through the sign-off below with the shift
  lead.

## The first runbooks

Between them, these cover every kind of equipment that at least four
rooms in five have in the [room inventory](../rooms/index.md), in all
four categories:

- [Projector shows no signal](../runbooks/av/rb-av-001-projector-shows-no-signal.md)
- [No sound from the room speakers](../runbooks/av/rb-av-007-no-sound-from-the-room-speakers.md)
- [Laptop shows no picture through the HDMI or USB-C cable](../runbooks/av/rb-av-012-laptop-shows-no-picture-through-the-hdmi-or-usb-c.md)
- [Lectern computer has no network connection](../runbooks/network/rb-net-001-lectern-computer-has-no-network-connection.md)
- [Wall port gives no network connection](../runbooks/network/rb-net-002-wall-port-gives-no-network-connection.md)
- [Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping in the classroom](../runbooks/network/rb-net-003-wi-fi-is-slow-or-keeps-dropping-in-the-classroom.md)
- [Wireless keyboard or mouse does not work](../runbooks/peripherals/rb-per-003-wireless-keyboard-or-mouse-does-not-work.md)
- [Instructor cannot sign in at the lectern computer](../runbooks/accounts/rb-acc-001-instructor-cannot-sign-in-at-the-lectern-computer.md)

Read the rest of each category during your first term.

## Sign-off

The shift lead signs a new technician off to take calls alone when they
can:

- find any room's equipment from its page in Rooms;
- follow each first runbook's Checks on the equipment, and say where each
  check leads if it fails;
- find in the escalation matrix who takes a fault in each kind of
  equipment, and say the urgency of a fault that stops a class;
- pack the kit bag, and write a handoff note the next shift can act on;
- state the safety rules and what is not a technician's to do;
- say why a technician never asks for, types or resets a password.

After sign-off, a technician who finds a runbook wrong or missing proposes
the change as [Contributing](../contributing/index.md) describes.
