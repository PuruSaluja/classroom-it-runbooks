# Escalation matrix

Who takes a fault that its runbook has not fixed, how urgent it is, and
what to hand on with it. Every runbook's Escalate section links this page
and names the role that takes its fault, as this page names it.

The owners below are roles, not named teams: a campus fills each one with
its own team and ticket queue. Phone numbers and queue names belong in the
team's contact list, not on this site.

## Safety comes first

If there is smoke, a burning smell, sparking, exposed wiring or water near
the equipment, or someone is hurt, leave the equipment alone, move people
away from it and call campus emergency services. Then tell the shift lead.
The ticket comes after.

## Who takes what

| What has failed | Takes it |
| --- | --- |
| Projectors, displays, speakers, microphones, assistive listening, switchers, extenders, control panels, document cameras, lecture capture appliances and cameras, and the room's AV cabling in walls and racks | [AV engineering](#av-engineering) |
| Power to the room or the lectern, lights, the screen's wall switch wiring, furniture | [Facilities](#facilities) |
| The lectern computer and lab workstations, their software, signing in, accounts, and replacement keyboards, mice and presenters | [Service desk](#service-desk) |
| Wall ports, the room's network switch, Wi-Fi, and a wireless presentation receiver that no device can reach | [Network team](#network-team) |
| Lecture capture schedules: a class that was never set to record | [Teaching-technology team](#teaching-technology-team) |
| Anything needing a decision: moving a class, the same fault in several rooms, an owner who has not answered | [Shift lead](#shift-lead) |

When a fault could belong to two owners, the runbook says which check
tells them apart. If it still is not clear, ask the shift lead rather
than sending the ticket to both.

## How urgent it is

| Urgency | When | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Now | A class in the room cannot go on: no picture or no sound in a lecture hall, assistive listening down for a student who needs it, no way to teach the session as planned | Assign the ticket to its owner and call the shift lead as well. Stay with the instructor until a workaround is in place or the lead takes over. |
| Today | The class can go on with a workaround (a portable speaker, the instructor's own laptop), or the next class in the room is later today | Assign the ticket to its owner, marked for today, and tell the instructor what happens next. |
| Planned | Nothing is affected before the room's next class on another day | Assign the ticket to its owner. |

A fault that comes back after it was fixed is one level more urgent than
it would be the first time: the runbook has not held.

## What to hand on

Every escalation carries, in the ticket:

- the room's identifier (`ALD-104`) and the equipment involved;
- the runbook's identifier and the last step you reached;
- what you saw at each check, and what you did;
- the workaround in place, if any, and whether the instructor knows;
- for a missed recording, the course, the date and the class's start and
  end.

Never put a password, PIN or code in a ticket, and never ask an instructor
or student for one. Account problems go to the service desk from the
person themselves.

## The roles

### Shift lead

The technician running the shift. Takes decisions the runbooks cannot:
moving a class to another room, calling in AV engineering urgently, and
anything waiting longer than its urgency allows, and makes sure open
tickets are handed to the next shift, as [Shift handoff](shift-handoff.md)
sets out.

### AV engineering

Repairs and replaces the room's audio-visual equipment, works at height
and inside racks and equipment, recovers recordings from lecture capture
appliances, and changes settings the runbooks tell technicians to leave
alone.

### Facilities

Power, lighting, furniture and the building itself. A room or lectern
with no power goes to facilities, not AV engineering, once the runbook's
checks show the equipment is not the cause.

### Service desk

Computers and accounts: the lectern computer and lab workstations, their
software and images, signing in, passwords and account locks, and
replacement input devices paired to a room. An instructor or student
with an account problem contacts the service desk themselves, so the
desk can confirm who they are.

### Network team

Wired and wireless networking: wall ports, the switches serving rooms and
labs, Wi-Fi coverage and the network that wireless presentation receivers
join.

### Teaching-technology team

Schedules lecture capture recordings and supports instructors in using
the room's teaching tools. A class that was never scheduled to record is
theirs; one that was scheduled and did not record goes to AV engineering.
