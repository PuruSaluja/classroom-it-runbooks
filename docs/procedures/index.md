# Procedures

How the team works around the runbooks: the escalation matrix, shift
handoff and ticket handling.

- [Escalation matrix](escalation-matrix.md): who takes a fault a runbook
  has not fixed, how urgent it is, and what to hand on with it. Every
  runbook's escalation step points here.
- [Shift handoff](shift-handoff.md): what to read and check at the start
  of a shift, the kit bag, keeping tickets current, and the handoff note
  that carries open work to the next shift.

Tickets are kept as each runbook's Close the ticket section says, and
escalated with what the matrix lists under what to hand on.
