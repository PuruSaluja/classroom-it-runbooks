# The last user is still signed in

The lectern computer or a lab workstation is still signed in as the
previous instructor or student, or is locked to their account, so the next
person cannot sign in. Their files and email may be open to anyone in the
room: treat it as a privacy problem, not only an inconvenience. If the next
person then cannot sign in, follow
[Instructor cannot sign in at the lectern computer](rb-acc-001-instructor-cannot-sign-in-at-the-lectern-computer.md).

## Symptoms

- "Someone else is still logged in", "it's locked with another professor's
  name".
- The previous user's desktop, email or files are on the screen.
- The lock screen shows another person's name.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 5 minutes.
- **Bring:** nothing.
- **Safety:** do not read, close or save anything in the previous user's
  session beyond what the steps say.
- **Do not:** save or close the previous user's files, read their email,
  or sign in as them. You may lose their unsaved work by signing them out;
  that is preferred to leaving their account open, and the steps say how
  to warn them.

## Checks

1. Look at the screen. **Expected:** the sign-in screen. If it shows the
   previous user's open desktop, go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix). If it shows a
   lock screen with their name, go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Sign the previous user out from the Start menu, without opening or
   saving anything; if the computer warns of unsaved work, choose to sign
   out anyway. **Expected:** the sign-in screen appears.
2. On the lock screen, choose **Other user** if it is offered, so the next
   person can sign in. If it is not offered, restart the computer from the
   lock screen's power menu, which signs the previous user out.
   **Expected:** the sign-in screen appears.
3. Note the previous user's name as it appeared, so the ticket can tell
   them that their session was signed out. **Expected:** the next person
   signs in.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
the service desk, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md),
if the computer cannot be signed out or restarted, or if it signs the same
person in again by itself.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced. Say that a session was signed
out and whose it was; the service desk tells them.
