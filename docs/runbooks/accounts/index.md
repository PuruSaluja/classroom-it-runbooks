# Account access

Instructors signing in at the lectern computer and to the room's systems,
and what a technician may do about it and what goes to the service desk.
Runbook identifiers in this category start `RB-ACC-`.

A technician never asks for, types or resets an instructor's password: the
runbooks here say how to tell a sign-in fault in the room from an account
problem, and how to hand the second to the service desk.

The runbooks are to come.
