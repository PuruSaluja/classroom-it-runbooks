# Wall port gives no network connection

A laptop or device plugged into one of the room's wall network ports gets
no connection. For the lectern computer's own link, follow
[Lectern computer has no network connection](rb-net-001-lectern-computer-has-no-network-connection.md).

## Symptoms

- "The network port by the window doesn't work", "my laptop says the cable
  is unplugged".
- No light at the laptop's network socket when it is plugged in.
- The laptop connects on Wi-Fi but not through the port.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes.
- **Bring:** a short known-good network cable and a laptop with a wired
  network port or adapter.
- **Safety:** floor and wall boxes may also carry mains power: plug into
  the network socket only, and do not open the box's cover beyond its lid.
- **Do not:** connect a switch, a router or two ports together, and do not
  patch cables in the network closet. Ports are activated and patched by
  the network team.

## Checks

1. Read the label on the port. **Expected:** a port number, as most ports
   have. Note it for the ticket. If the port has no label or is marked
   inactive, go to [Escalate](#escalate).
2. Plug your laptop into the port with your known-good cable. **Expected:**
   the light at the laptop's socket comes on, and the laptop connects. If it
   does, the fault was the user's cable or device: go to step 1 of
   [Fix](#fix).
3. Plug your laptop into another port in the room. **Expected:** it
   connects. If it does, the first port is faulty or not active: go to
   step 2 of [Fix](#fix). If no port works, go to [Escalate](#escalate).

## Fix

1. Give the user the known-good cable to use, or show them how to seat the
   cable until it clicks. **Expected:** their device connects.
2. Move the user to a working port, and put a "Do not use: reported" label
   on the faulty one, with the date. **Expected:** the user is connected
   and the faulty port is marked.
3. Escalate the faulty port with its number.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
the network team, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/escalation-matrix.md),
with each faulty port's number and whether any port in the room worked.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
