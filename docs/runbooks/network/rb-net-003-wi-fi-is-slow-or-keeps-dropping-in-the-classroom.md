# Wi-Fi is slow or keeps dropping in the classroom

Laptops and phones in the room connect to the campus Wi-Fi but are slow,
or lose the connection again and again. If one device cannot join the
Wi-Fi at all because of its sign-in, refer its owner to the service desk;
for a wired connection, follow
[Wall port gives no network connection](rb-net-002-wall-port-gives-no-network-connection.md).

## Symptoms

- "The Wi-Fi in here is terrible", "the quiz keeps kicking students out".
- Many devices in the room lose the connection or load pages slowly,
  often at the start of a class when everyone joins at once.
- Devices show the campus network with one bar, or switch to another
  network.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 15 minutes.
- **Bring:** your phone or laptop, with a speed-test or Wi-Fi analyzer app
  approved by the network team.
- **Safety:** the room's access points are on the ceiling: look at them
  from the floor; nothing here needs a ladder.
- **Do not:** set up a hotspot or a personal router in the room, change an
  access point's settings, or unplug one. Access points are managed by the
  network team.

## Checks

1. Ask how many devices are affected. **Expected:** several. If only one
   device is, the fault is likely that device: go to step 1 of
   [Fix](#fix).
2. Look at the room's access point lights, from the floor. **Expected:**
   steady, in their normal color. If an access point has no light or a red
   one, go to [Escalate](#escalate).
3. Stand in the middle of the room and look at the network your device has
   joined. **Expected:** the campus network, with strong signal. If it
   joined a guest or other network, go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
4. Run the approved speed test in the middle and at the back of the room.
   **Expected:** pages load quickly and the test completes in both places.
   If it is slow or fails, go to step 3 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Ask the user to turn their device's Wi-Fi off and on, or to forget the
   campus network and join it again. **Expected:** it connects and stays
   connected. If it does not, refer them to the service desk.
2. Join the campus network, not a guest one, and move off any network
   left over from another building. **Expected:** full speed.
3. Record the speed test results, the time, the number of people in the
   room and where you stood. Then escalate. **Expected:** the ticket holds
   enough for the network team to compare with their own records.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
the network team, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md),
with the speed test results, when in the class it happens, and how many
people were in the room.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
