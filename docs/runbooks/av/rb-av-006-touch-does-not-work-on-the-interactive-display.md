# Touch does not work on the interactive display

The interactive touch display shows the picture, but touching it does
nothing, or the touch lands in the wrong place. If it shows no picture at
all, follow [Display shows no picture](rb-av-005-display-shows-no-picture.md).

## Symptoms

- "I can see my screen but I can't write on it", "touching it clicks
  somewhere else".
- Touches do not move the pointer, or the pointer jumps to a point away
  from the finger or pen.
- Touch works on the display's own whiteboard but not on the computer's
  picture, or the other way round.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes.
- **Bring:** a microfiber cloth.
- **Safety:** clean the screen only with a dry or slightly damp cloth, never
  a spray on the screen itself.
- **Do not:** open the display or take it off its stand or mount, and do
  not install drivers or software on the lectern computer. Software is for
  the service desk.

## Checks

1. Look at the screen's surface and its edges. **Expected:** nothing on the
   screen or resting on its frame: tape, a cable, a marker, a book. Objects
   on the frame block touch. If something is there, go to step 1 of
   [Fix](#fix).
2. Open the display's own whiteboard or home screen and draw a line.
   **Expected:** the line follows your finger. If it does, the display's
   touch works and the fault is the connection to the computer: go to step
   2 of [Fix](#fix). If it does not, go to step 4 of [Fix](#fix).
3. On the computer's picture, touch an icon. **Expected:** the icon is
   chosen where you touch. If touches land elsewhere, go to step 3 of
   [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Clear the screen and its frame, and wipe the screen with the cloth.
   **Expected:** touches register again.
2. Check that the display's USB touch cable is plugged in at the computer
   or the lectern's USB hub: unplug it and plug it back in firmly.
   **Expected:** after a few seconds, touches on the computer's picture
   register.
3. On the display, select the input that matches the computer's
   connection, so the touch goes to the same computer as the picture.
   **Expected:** touches land where you touch.
4. Turn the display off with its power button, wait 30 seconds, and turn
   it on. **Expected:** touch works on its whiteboard. If not, escalate.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/escalation-matrix.md),
or to the service desk if touch works on the display's whiteboard but the
computer reports a problem with the touch device.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
