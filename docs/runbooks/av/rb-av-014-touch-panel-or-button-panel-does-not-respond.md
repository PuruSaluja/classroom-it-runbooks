# Touch panel or button panel does not respond

The room's touch panel or wall button panel is dark, frozen, or shows its
buttons but nothing happens when they are pressed. If the panel works and
only the projector does not turn on, follow
[Projector will not turn on](rb-av-002-projector-will-not-turn-on.md).

## Symptoms

- "The control screen is frozen", "none of the buttons do anything".
- The touch panel is black, shows a logo or a "connecting" or "offline"
  message, or stays on one page.
- A button panel's lights do not change when a button is pressed.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes.
- **Bring:** nothing; the projector's and displays' own remotes are useful,
  if the room keeps them in the lectern drawer.
- **Safety:** nothing beyond the room's usual care.
- **Do not:** open the rack, restart the control processor from inside it,
  or load a program onto the panel. Control programming is for AV
  engineering.

## Checks

1. Touch a blank part of the touch panel's screen once, which only wakes
   it, or look at the button panel's lights. **Expected:** the touch panel
   shows its home page, or the button panel's buttons are lit. If the
   touch panel stays black, or no button is lit, go to step 1 of
   [Fix](#fix).
2. Look for a message on the touch panel. **Expected:** none. "Offline",
   "connecting" or "no control" means the panel cannot reach the control
   processor: go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
3. Compare what the panel shows with the room. **Expected:** the projector
   or display it shows as on is on, showing the source it shows as
   selected. If they differ, the panel's commands are not reaching the
   equipment: go to step 3 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Press the plug of the panel's cable (underneath or behind the panel) in
   firmly, without pulling it. **Expected:** the panel lights within a
   minute.
2. Wait two minutes, as a panel reconnects on its own after a short network
   drop. **Expected:** the message clears and the buttons work.
3. For the class, turn the projector or display on with its own remote and
   choose its input there. **Expected:** the class can go on. Then escalate:
   the panel still needs AV engineering.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md).
Say what the panel showed, and whether the equipment worked from its own
remote.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
