# Cannot share a screen wirelessly

A laptop, tablet or phone cannot share its screen to the room through the
wireless presentation receiver, or the shared picture freezes. If a laptop
plugged in by cable shows nothing either, follow
[Laptop shows no picture through the HDMI or USB-C cable](rb-av-012-laptop-shows-no-picture-through-the-hdmi-or-usb-c.md).

## Symptoms

- "I can't connect to the screen", "it says it can't find the room".
- The device does not list the room's receiver, or asks for a code and then
  fails.
- The shared picture appears, then freezes or lags far behind.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes.
- **Bring:** a laptop of your own that has shared to a receiver before.
- **Safety:** nothing beyond the room's usual care.
- **Do not:** change the receiver's network or security settings, or ask
  for or write down any network password. The receiver's settings are for
  AV engineering; network access is for the network team.

## Checks

1. Select the wireless source on the touch panel or button panel.
   **Expected:** the screen shows the receiver's welcome page, with the
   room's name and how to connect. If it does not, go to step 1 of
   [Fix](#fix).
2. Compare the room name on the welcome page with the one the device is
   trying to reach. **Expected:** the same room. Neighboring rooms'
   receivers can show in the list; if it is another room's, go to step 2 of
   [Fix](#fix).
3. Look at which network the device is on. **Expected:** the network the
   welcome page names (usually the campus Wi-Fi). A device on a guest or
   phone network cannot reach the receiver: go to step 3 of [Fix](#fix).
4. Share from your own laptop. **Expected:** your screen appears. If it
   does, the fault is the instructor's device: go to step 4 of [Fix](#fix).
   If it does not, go to step 5 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Select the wireless source again and wait 30 seconds. **Expected:** the
   welcome page appears.
2. Connect to the room's own receiver, by the name on its welcome page, and
   enter the code shown on the screen if it asks. **Expected:** the shared
   screen appears.
3. Move the device to the network the welcome page names, then connect
   again. **Expected:** the shared screen appears.
4. Close and reopen the sharing app or the screen-sharing menu on the
   device and try once more. **Expected:** the screen appears. If not, offer
   the room's cable and refer the instructor to the service desk for their
   device.
5. Turn the room's system off from the panel, wait a minute, turn it on
   and select the wireless source. **Expected:** your laptop's screen
   appears. If it does not, escalate.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md),
or to the network team if no device can reach a receiver that shows its
welcome page. Say which devices were tried and what each one showed.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
