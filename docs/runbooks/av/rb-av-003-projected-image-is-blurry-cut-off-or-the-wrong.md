# Projected image is blurry, cut off or the wrong shape

The projector shows the picture, but it is out of focus, runs off the
screen, is stretched or squashed, or is wider at one edge than the other.
If there is no picture at all, follow
[Projector shows no signal](rb-av-001-projector-shows-no-signal.md); if the
screen itself is stuck, follow
[Projection screen will not come down or go up](rb-av-004-projection-screen-will-not-come-down-or-go-up.md).

## Symptoms

- "The picture is fuzzy", "the edges of my slides are cut off", "everything
  looks stretched".
- The image spills past the screen's border, does not fill it, or is a
  trapezoid rather than a rectangle.
- Text is blurred on the screen but sharp on the computer's own monitor.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 15 minutes.
- **Bring:** a laptop, to compare its picture with the lectern computer's.
- **Safety:** do not look into the lens. Focus, zoom and lens shift on a
  ceiling projector are adjusted from the floor with its remote or the
  touch panel, never from a ladder.
- **Do not:** move the projector or its mount, or change its settings
  beyond the picture settings below. A moved projector needs AV
  engineering to set it up again.

## Checks

1. Look at the screen. **Expected:** it is all the way down, flat, and not
   swinging. If it is partly down, go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
2. Compare the computer's monitor with the screen. **Expected:** both show
   the same picture with the same edges. If only the shape or the cut-off
   edges differ, go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
3. On the computer, open the display settings and look at the resolution
   for the projector's output. **Expected:** the recommended resolution, as
   the settings mark it. If another one is set, go to step 3 of [Fix](#fix).
4. Show a slide or page with small text and look at it from the middle of
   the room. **Expected:** the text is sharp across the whole image. If it
   is blurred all over, go to step 4 of [Fix](#fix); if it is sharp in the
   middle and blurred at one side, go to step 5 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Raise the screen and lower it again all the way from the panel or the
   wall switch, and let it settle. **Expected:** the screen hangs flat and
   the image falls inside its border.
2. On the projector's remote or the touch panel, choose the picture's
   **Aspect** setting that matches the source (usually **Auto** or **16:9**).
   **Expected:** the picture fills the screen without stretching.
3. Set the computer's output to the recommended resolution. **Expected:**
   the whole desktop appears on the screen, with nothing cut off.
4. Use the projector's **Focus** control (on the remote or the panel) until
   the small text is sharp. **Expected:** sharp text across the image.
5. If the image is sharp in one part only, or is a trapezoid, the projector
   or screen has moved. Do not adjust keystone or lens shift: escalate.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md),
with a photograph of the screen showing the problem.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
