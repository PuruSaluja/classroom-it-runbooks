# Assistive listening receiver is silent

A student using the room's assistive listening system hears nothing, or
only hiss, through their receiver while the room's speakers work. This is
an accessibility fault: handle it before routine work. If the room's
speakers are silent too, follow
[No sound from the room speakers](rb-av-007-no-sound-from-the-room-speakers.md)
first.

## Symptoms

- "My listening device isn't picking anything up", reported by the student,
  the instructor or disability services.
- The receiver's light is off, or on but with no sound in its earpiece.
- Hiss or another room's sound in the earpiece.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes.
- **Bring:** a spare charged receiver and earpiece from the AV store, and
  new earpiece covers.
- **Safety:** give the student a fresh earpiece cover; do not use an
  earpiece someone else has worn without one.
- **Do not:** change the transmitter's channel. It is set so that
  neighboring rooms do not overlap.

## Checks

1. Look at the receiver's power light. **Expected:** on. If it is off, go
   to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
2. Compare the receiver's channel with the one on the label beside the
   room's transmitter (in the rack or on the lectern). **Expected:** the
   same channel. If not, go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
3. Look at the transmitter's lights. **Expected:** its power and audio
   lights are lit, and the audio light flickers when the room's
   microphone is used. If not, go to [Escalate](#escalate).
4. Listen through the spare receiver while someone speaks into the room's
   microphone. **Expected:** clear sound. If the spare works, go to step 3
   of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Charge the receiver or put in fresh batteries, and turn it on.
   **Expected:** its light comes on and the student hears the room.
2. Set the receiver to the transmitter's channel. **Expected:** clear
   sound.
3. Give the student the spare receiver for the class, and take the faulty
   one out of use with a label. **Expected:** the student hears the room.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md),
as urgent, since a student cannot follow the class without it. Tell the
instructor what has been done.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
