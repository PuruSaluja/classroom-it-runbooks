# Remote participants cannot hear the room

In a hybrid class, people joining the video meeting from elsewhere cannot
hear the instructor or the students in the room, though the room can hear
them. If the room cannot hear the remote participants, follow
[No sound from the room speakers](rb-av-007-no-sound-from-the-room-speakers.md);
if the instructor's microphone is not heard in the room either, follow
[Microphone cannot be heard in the room](rb-av-008-microphone-cannot-be-heard-in-the-room.md).

## Symptoms

- "The students online say they can't hear us", "they only hear me when I
  stand at the computer".
- The meeting shows the room's microphone as muted, or its level meter
  does not move when people in the room speak.
- Remote participants hear the room faintly or with an echo.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes, during a break in the class if you can.
- **Bring:** your phone, to join the meeting as a remote participant and
  listen.
- **Safety:** nothing beyond the room's usual care.
- **Do not:** change the audio processor's settings, or move the ceiling
  microphone. Its place and settings are set up for the room.

## Checks

1. Look at the meeting window on the lectern computer. **Expected:** the
   microphone is not muted in the meeting. If it is, go to step 1 of
   [Fix](#fix).
2. Open the meeting app's audio settings and look at the selected
   microphone. **Expected:** the room's audio system (usually named after
   the audio processor, or "USB audio"), not the computer's built-in or a
   webcam's microphone. If another one is selected, go to step 2 of
   [Fix](#fix).
3. Look at the light on the ceiling microphone array, from the floor.
   **Expected:** steady green, or its normal color. If it is red, the room
   has muted it: go to step 3 of [Fix](#fix).
4. Join the meeting from your phone, with its sound on and the phone's
   microphone muted, and have someone in the room speak. **Expected:** you
   hear them clearly. If you do not, go to step 4 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Unmute the microphone in the meeting. **Expected:** the meeting's level
   meter moves when someone in the room speaks.
2. Select the room's audio system as both the meeting's microphone and its
   speaker. **Expected:** the level meter moves, and remote participants
   are heard through the ceiling speakers.
3. Unmute the room's microphones on the touch panel (the **Mic mute** or
   **Privacy** button). **Expected:** the ceiling microphone's light turns
   green.
4. Leave the meeting and join it again on the lectern computer, selecting
   the room's audio system when it asks. **Expected:** your phone hears the
   room. If it still does not, escalate.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/escalation-matrix.md).
Say which meeting app was used, which microphone it showed, and what the
ceiling microphone's light showed.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
