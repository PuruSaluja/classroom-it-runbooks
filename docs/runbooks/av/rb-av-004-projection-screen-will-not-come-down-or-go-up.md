# Projection screen will not come down or go up

The motorized projection screen does not move when it is lowered or
raised from the touch panel, the button panel or its wall switch, or it
stops part of the way. If the screen is down but the picture on it is
wrong, follow
[Projected image is blurry, cut off or the wrong shape](rb-av-003-projected-image-is-blurry-cut-off-or-the-wrong.md).

## Symptoms

- "The screen won't come down", "the screen is stuck halfway".
- Pressing **Screen down** or **Screen up** does nothing, or the motor hums
  without the screen moving.
- The screen is crooked, or its fabric is caught or torn.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes.
- **Bring:** nothing.
- **Safety:** keep clear of the screen while its motor runs, and never pull
  the screen by hand: it can tear or come off its roller. Nothing in this
  runbook needs a ladder.
- **Do not:** touch the screen's case, wiring or limit settings. If the
  screen is torn or hangs crooked, stop at the first check and escalate.

## Checks

1. Look at the screen from the floor. **Expected:** the fabric is whole and
   hangs straight. If it is torn, caught or crooked, do not run the motor:
   go to [Escalate](#escalate).
2. Find the screen's wall switch, if the room has one (usually beside the
   whiteboard or the door). **Expected:** it is set to the middle, stopped,
   position, or the room has none: go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix). A switch
   left at **Up** or **Down** can stop the panel's buttons working: go to
   step 1 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Set the wall switch to its middle position and wait 5 seconds.
   **Expected:** the switch stays in the middle. Go on to the next step.
2. Press **Screen down** on the touch panel or button panel once, and
   listen at the screen. **Expected:** the screen comes down and stops at
   its usual place. If you hear the motor but the screen does not move,
   stop and escalate. If you hear nothing, go to the next step.
3. Use the wall switch instead: set it to **Down**, and back to the middle
   once the screen is down. **Expected:** the screen comes down. If it does,
   the panel's control of the screen has failed: leave the screen down for
   the class and escalate after it.
4. If neither moves the screen, escalate. If a projector shows on the wall,
   the class can go on while the screen is fixed.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md).
Say whether the motor could be heard, and whether the wall switch worked.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
