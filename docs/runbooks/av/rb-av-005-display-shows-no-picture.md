# Display shows no picture

A flat-panel display (on the wall, or at a team table) or the confidence
monitor (the screen facing the instructor) is dark or shows "No signal"
while the room's other screens work. If every screen in the room is blank,
follow [Projector shows no signal](rb-av-001-projector-shows-no-signal.md)
first, as the fault is then nearer the source; for a touch screen that
shows a picture but ignores touch, follow
[Touch does not work on the interactive display](rb-av-006-touch-does-not-work-on-the-interactive-display.md).

## Symptoms

- "The TV on our table is blank", "the screen in front of me is off".
- The display is dark, shows "No signal" or "Check cable", or shows its
  own menu or a different input from the others.
- The display's power light is off, red, or blinking.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes.
- **Bring:** a laptop with HDMI and USB-C outputs, and a known-good HDMI
  cable.
- **Safety:** wall displays are heavy and mounted: do not pull on one or
  lean on it. Reach the buttons on its edge from the floor; if you cannot,
  use its remote.
- **Do not:** take a display off its mount or open it, and do not change
  its settings beyond its input and power.

## Checks

1. Look at the display's power light. **Expected:** steady, in its "on"
   color. If it is off or shows standby, go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
2. If the display shows a message or menu, read it. **Expected:** no
   message. "No signal" or an input name means it is on the wrong input or
   not receiving: go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
3. At a team table, plug your laptop into the table's input. At the
   confidence monitor, select the lectern computer on the touch panel.
   **Expected:** the picture appears on the display. If it appears on the
   room's other screens but not this one, go to step 3 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Press the power button on the display's edge or its remote once.
   **Expected:** the display comes on within a few seconds.
2. Press **Input** (or **Source**) on the remote or the display's edge and
   choose the input the room uses for it, usually the one marked HDMI 1 or
   the one the other displays show. **Expected:** the picture appears.
3. Turn the display off with its power button, wait 30 seconds, and turn it
   on. **Expected:** it comes on and shows the picture. If not, the display
   or the cable behind it has failed: escalate.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/escalation-matrix.md).
Say which display (wall, table number or confidence monitor), what its
power light shows and what message it gives.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
