# Laptop shows no picture through the HDMI or USB-C cable

An instructor's or a student's laptop is plugged into the lectern's or a
table's HDMI or USB-C cable, but its picture does not appear, while the
lectern computer's does. If nothing appears from any source, follow
[Projector shows no signal](rb-av-001-projector-shows-no-signal.md); if the
laptop's picture appears but flickers or drops out, follow
[Picture flickers, sparkles or drops out](rb-av-013-picture-flickers-sparkles-or-drops-out.md).

## Symptoms

- "My laptop won't show up on the screen", "it works for the computer but
  not for me".
- The screen shows "No signal" once the laptop source is selected.
- The laptop's own screen flickers once when the cable is plugged in, or
  shows nothing different.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes.
- **Bring:** USB-C to HDMI and Mini DisplayPort to HDMI adapters, and a
  laptop of your own to compare.
- **Safety:** nothing beyond the room's usual care.
- **Do not:** install software or drivers on the instructor's laptop, or
  change its settings beyond its display output. Their laptop is theirs;
  for anything more, refer them to the service desk.

## Checks

1. Look at the touch panel or button panel, or the table's button.
   **Expected:** the laptop source (the lectern's or that table's input) is
   selected. If not, go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
2. Look at both ends of the cable. **Expected:** seated fully; for USB-C,
   the cable's own end, not an adapter the room does not use. If loose, go
   to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
3. On the laptop, open its display settings. **Expected:** a second display
   is listed, set to duplicate or extend. If none is listed, go to step 3 of
   [Fix](#fix); if it is listed but the laptop is set to show only its own
   screen, go to step 4 of [Fix](#fix).
4. Plug your own laptop into the same cable and select the same source.
   **Expected:** your picture appears. If it does, the fault is the
   instructor's laptop or its port: go to step 5 of [Fix](#fix). If it does
   not, the fault is the room's cable: go to [Escalate](#escalate).

## Fix

1. Select the laptop source. **Expected:** the picture appears within about
   10 seconds.
2. Unplug the cable at the laptop and plug it in firmly. **Expected:** the
   laptop's screen flickers once and the picture appears.
3. On a USB-C laptop, plug the cable into another USB-C port; some carry no
   video. If the laptop has HDMI, use the HDMI cable. **Expected:** the
   laptop lists a second display and the picture appears.
4. Set the laptop to duplicate or extend its screen (on Windows, press the
   Windows key and P; on a Mac, use Displays in System Settings).
   **Expected:** the picture appears on the room's screen.
5. Try the adapter that fits the laptop's port, or the room's wireless
   presentation if it has one. **Expected:** the picture appears. If none
   works, the laptop's port is the fault: refer the instructor to the
   service desk and offer the lectern computer for the class.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/escalation-matrix.md),
when the room's cable fails with a known-good laptop. Say which input
(lectern or table number) and which connector failed.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
