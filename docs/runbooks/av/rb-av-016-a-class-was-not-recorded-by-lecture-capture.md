# A class was not recorded by lecture capture

An instructor expected a class to be recorded by the room's lecture
capture appliance and camera, and it was not, or the recording has no
picture or no sound. Report it the same day: a missed recording can
sometimes be recovered from the appliance, but only before it is
overwritten, and only by AV engineering. If the room's microphone was not
working during the class, follow
[Microphone cannot be heard in the room](rb-av-008-microphone-cannot-be-heard-in-the-room.md)
for that fault as well.

## Symptoms

- "My lecture didn't record", "the recording is just a black screen", "the
  recording has no sound".
- The appliance's light was not lit, or not in its recording color, during
  the class.
- The recording is missing from the course's video page.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes in the room; the recording itself is
  escalated.
- **Bring:** the class's date, start time and course, from the ticket.
- **Safety:** the camera is mounted on the wall or ceiling: do not reach
  for it or move it.
- **Do not:** restart or unplug the appliance, which can lose a recording
  still on it, and do not schedule or change recordings. Schedules belong
  to the teaching-technology team.

## Checks

1. Look at the appliance's status light (on the lectern or the wall
   plate). **Expected:** lit in its idle color, showing it is on and ready.
   If it is off or red, go to [Escalate](#escalate) without touching it.
2. Look at the touch panel's capture page, if it has one. **Expected:** the
   next scheduled recording is shown. If it shows none, the class may never
   have been scheduled: go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
3. Look at the camera from the floor. **Expected:** it points at the front
   of the room and is not covered. If it is covered or turned away, go to
   step 2 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Tell the instructor that recordings are scheduled by the
   teaching-technology team, and pass the ticket to them with the course
   and times. **Expected:** the ticket shows the team as its owner.
2. Uncover the camera if its cover is within reach without a ladder.
   **Expected:** the capture page's preview, if it has one, shows the front
   of the room. If the camera is turned away, do not move it: escalate.
3. Escalate the missing recording itself, with the class's date, time and
   course, so AV engineering can look for it on the appliance.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/escalation-matrix.md),
the same day, with the course, the date and the class's start and end.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
