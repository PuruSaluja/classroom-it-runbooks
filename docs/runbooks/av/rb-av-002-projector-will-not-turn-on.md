# Projector will not turn on

The projector stays dark when it is turned on from the touch panel or button
panel. If it turns on but shows no picture, follow
[Projector shows no signal](rb-av-001-projector-shows-no-signal.md).

## Symptoms

- "I pressed on and nothing happened", "the projector is dead".
- No image, no fan noise, and no light on the projector after pressing
  **On**, or a light that blinks in a colour other than its usual one.
- The panel may show the projector as warming up, or as on.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes, plus the projector's cool-down.
- **Bring:** nothing beyond your phone, to photograph the projector's lights.
- **Safety:** do not look into the lens. Ceiling projectors are out of
  reach: look at their lights from the floor; nothing here needs a ladder.
- **Do not:** pull the projector's power cable or turn off a circuit
  breaker, and do not open the projector. Power is for facilities; the
  projector's inside is for AV engineering.

## Checks

1. Press **On** (or the projector's power button on the panel) once and
   wait a full minute. **Expected:** the projector's fan starts and the
   image appears within about 30 seconds. A projector that was just turned
   off will not start again until it has cooled; if it was, go to step 1 of
   [Fix](#fix).
2. Look at the lights on the projector, from the floor. **Expected:** a
   steady light that shows the projector is on or in standby. Photograph
   any blinking or red light and go to step 3 of [Fix](#fix). If no light is
   lit at all, go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
3. Look at the other equipment in the room: the switcher's lights and the
   lectern computer. **Expected:** they are on. If nothing in the room has
   power, go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Wait for the cool-down: about two minutes after the projector was turned
   off. Then press **On** once. **Expected:** the fan starts and the image
   appears.
2. If the projector shows no light, or nothing in the room has power, do
   not go further: the fault is power. Escalate to facilities for the room's
   power and to AV engineering for the projector.
3. If a light is blinking or red, turn the projector off from the panel,
   wait for the fan to stop, and turn it on once more. **Expected:** it
   starts normally. If the same light comes back, it is a fault only AV
   engineering can clear: escalate with the photograph.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, or to facilities when the room has no power, through the
[escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md). Attach the photograph of the
projector's lights.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
