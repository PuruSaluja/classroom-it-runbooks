# Runbooks

One fault per runbook: its symptoms, the checks in order, the fix, and when
to escalate. Each runbook has an identifier made of its category's prefix
and a number, such as `RB-AV-001`, which tickets and the knowledge base
quote.

| Category | Prefix | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Audio-visual](av/index.md) | AV | Projectors, displays, audio, cabling and room control systems |
| [Network](network/index.md) | NET | Wall ports, the lectern's network link and Wi-Fi |
| [Peripherals](peripherals/index.md) | PER | Document cameras, keyboards and mice, presenters, webcams, USB hubs |
| [Account access](accounts/index.md) | ACC | Signing in at the lectern and to the room's systems |

If a runbook's last step does not fix the fault, follow its escalation
step and [Procedures](../procedures/index.md).
