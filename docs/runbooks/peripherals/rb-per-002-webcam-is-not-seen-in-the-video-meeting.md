# Webcam is not seen in the video meeting

In a hybrid class, the room's webcam does not show in the video meeting on
the lectern computer: the meeting shows a black box, the instructor's
initials, or the wrong camera. For the room's sound in the meeting, follow
[Remote participants cannot hear the room](../av/rb-av-009-remote-participants-cannot-hear-the-room.md).

## Symptoms

- "The students online can't see me", "the camera is black".
- The meeting's camera button shows the camera as off, or its video shows
  the document camera or nothing.
- The webcam's light is off when the meeting's video is on.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 10 minutes.
- **Bring:** nothing.
- **Safety:** the webcam may be mounted above the screen: look at it from
  the floor, and do not reach for it.
- **Do not:** install drivers or camera software, or change the computer's
  privacy settings beyond turning the camera on for the meeting app. Those
  are for the service desk.

## Checks

1. Look at the meeting's camera button. **Expected:** the camera is on. If
   it is off, go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
2. Open the meeting app's video settings and look at the selected camera.
   **Expected:** the room's webcam (named after its model, not "virtual"
   and not the document camera). If another is selected, go to step 2 of
   [Fix](#fix); if the room's webcam is not listed, go to step 3 of
   [Fix](#fix).
3. Look at the webcam. **Expected:** its privacy shutter is open and its
   light is on while the meeting's video is on. If the shutter is closed,
   go to step 4 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Turn the camera on in the meeting. **Expected:** the room appears in the
   meeting's video.
2. Select the room's webcam in the meeting's video settings. **Expected:**
   the room appears.
3. Check the webcam's USB cable at the lectern's USB hub or the computer:
   unplug it and plug it back in firmly, then leave the meeting and join
   again. **Expected:** the webcam is listed and the room appears.
4. Slide the shutter open. **Expected:** the room appears. If the webcam is
   still not seen, escalate.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
the service desk if the computer does not list the webcam, or to AV
engineering if it is listed but shows nothing, through the
[escalation matrix](../../procedures/escalation-matrix.md).

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
