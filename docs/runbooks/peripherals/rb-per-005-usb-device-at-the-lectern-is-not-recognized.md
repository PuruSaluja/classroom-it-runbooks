# USB device at the lectern is not recognized

A USB drive, a clicker's receiver or another device plugged into the
lectern's USB hub does not appear on the lectern computer. For a webcam
the meeting does not see, follow
[Webcam is not seen in the video meeting](rb-per-002-webcam-is-not-seen-in-the-video-meeting.md);
for the wireless keyboard and mouse, follow
[Wireless keyboard or mouse does not work](rb-per-003-wireless-keyboard-or-mouse-does-not-work.md).

## Symptoms

- "My USB stick won't show up", "nothing happens when I plug it in".
- The computer does not show the drive, or shows "USB device not
  recognized".
- Several devices on the hub stop working at once.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 5 minutes.
- **Bring:** a USB drive of your own that you know works, with nothing on
  it you would mind the computer reading.
- **Safety:** nothing beyond the room's usual care.
- **Do not:** open files on an instructor's drive, or run anything from it.
  If the computer warns that a drive is unsafe, stop and escalate to the
  service desk.

## Checks

1. Look at the hub's power light, if it has one. **Expected:** on. If it is
   off, go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
2. Plug your own drive into the same hub port. **Expected:** it appears on
   the computer. If it does, the fault is the instructor's device: go to
   step 2 of [Fix](#fix). If it does not, go to step 3 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Check the hub's power plug and its cable to the computer: press both in
   firmly. **Expected:** the hub's light comes on and devices appear.
2. Try the instructor's device in a USB port on the computer itself. If it
   still does not appear, the device or its format is the fault: suggest
   the instructor uploads the file to their cloud storage from another
   computer, and refer them to the service desk. **Expected:** the
   instructor has a way to show the file.
3. Plug the device into a USB port on the computer itself. **Expected:** it
   appears. If it does, the hub has failed: escalate. If it does not,
   restart the computer once the instructor has saved their work, and try
   again.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
the service desk, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md),
for a failed hub or a computer that recognizes no USB device.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
