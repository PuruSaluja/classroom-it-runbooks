# Wireless presenter does not change slides

The room's wireless presenter (the clicker) does not move the slides
forward or back, or its laser pointer does not light. If the lectern
computer itself does not respond to its keyboard or mouse, follow
[Wireless keyboard or mouse does not work](rb-per-003-wireless-keyboard-or-mouse-does-not-work.md).

## Symptoms

- "The clicker doesn't work", "my slides won't advance".
- Pressing the presenter's buttons does nothing on the screen.
- The presenter's light does not come on when a button is pressed.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 5 minutes.
- **Bring:** fresh AAA batteries.
- **Safety:** never point the presenter's laser at anyone's eyes, when
  testing it or otherwise.
- **Do not:** take a presenter from another room; each is paired to its
  room's receiver.

## Checks

1. Press a button and watch the presenter's light. **Expected:** it lights.
   If not, go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
2. Look for the presenter's USB receiver in the lectern computer or its USB
   hub (it may be stored in the presenter itself). **Expected:** plugged in.
   If not, go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
3. Click once on the slide show window on the computer, then press the
   presenter's forward button. **Expected:** the slide advances. If it does,
   the slides were not the active window: go to step 3 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Replace the presenter's batteries. **Expected:** its light comes on when
   a button is pressed.
2. Plug the receiver into the computer or the hub. **Expected:** within a
   few seconds, the presenter changes slides.
3. Show the instructor that the slide show must be the active window: click
   on it once after switching from another program. **Expected:** the
   presenter changes slides. If none of this works, escalate.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
the service desk, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md),
for a replacement presenter paired to the room.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
