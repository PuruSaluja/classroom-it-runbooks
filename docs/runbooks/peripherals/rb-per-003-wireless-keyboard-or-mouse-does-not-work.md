# Wireless keyboard or mouse does not work

The lectern's wireless keyboard or mouse does nothing, or works only now
and then: the pointer does not move, or typing does not appear. If the
lectern computer's screen is dark, follow
[Projector shows no signal](../av/rb-av-001-projector-shows-no-signal.md)
first, as the computer may be asleep.

## Symptoms

- "The mouse is dead", "the keyboard isn't typing".
- The pointer freezes or jumps; keys are missed or repeated.
- The device's light does not come on when it is switched on.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 5 minutes.
- **Bring:** fresh AA and AAA batteries.
- **Safety:** nothing beyond the room's usual care.
- **Do not:** pair the keyboard or mouse to another computer, or take a
  set from another room. Sets are paired to their room's receiver.

## Checks

1. Look at the device's power switch, usually underneath. **Expected:** set
   to on. If it is off, go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
2. Move the mouse or press a key, and watch the device's light, if it has
   one. **Expected:** the light comes on, steady. If it does not, or blinks
   red, go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
3. Look for the small USB receiver in the computer or the lectern's USB
   hub. **Expected:** it is plugged in. If it is missing or loose, go to
   step 3 of [Fix](#fix).
4. Use the device close to the lectern. **Expected:** it works. If it works
   only close by, go to step 4 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Switch the device on. **Expected:** it works within a few seconds.
2. Replace its batteries. **Expected:** its light comes on and it works.
3. Plug the receiver in firmly, in a USB port on the computer itself if the
   hub's ports do not work. **Expected:** within a few seconds, the device
   works.
4. Move metal objects and other wireless devices away from the receiver,
   or plug the receiver into a port nearer the front of the lectern.
   **Expected:** the device works across the lectern. If it still does not,
   leave a wired keyboard and mouse from the AV store if the room has none,
   and escalate.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
the service desk, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md),
for a replacement set paired to the room.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
