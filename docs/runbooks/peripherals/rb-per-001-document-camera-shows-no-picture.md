# Document camera shows no picture

The document camera is selected, but the room's screen shows nothing, a
black image, or "No signal". If no source shows a picture, follow
[Projector shows no signal](../av/rb-av-001-projector-shows-no-signal.md);
if the picture appears but is blurred, the camera's focus is the fault:
press its **Auto focus** button.

## Symptoms

- "The doc cam isn't working", "I can't show my notes".
- The screen is black or shows "No signal" when the document camera source
  is chosen.
- The camera's lamp or head light is off.

## Before you start

- **Time:** about 5 minutes.
- **Bring:** nothing.
- **Safety:** do not look straight into the camera's light. Fold the arm
  only the way it is made to fold.
- **Do not:** open the camera, or update its firmware.

## Checks

1. Look at the touch panel or button panel. **Expected:** the document
   camera source is selected. If not, go to step 1 of [Fix](#fix).
2. Look at the camera's power light. **Expected:** on, in its normal color.
   If it is off, go to step 2 of [Fix](#fix).
3. Look at the camera's arm and head. **Expected:** unfolded, with the lens
   cap off and the head pointing down at the desk. If not, go to step 3 of
   [Fix](#fix).
4. Look at the camera's own output buttons, if it has them. **Expected:**
   the camera is sending its picture out (often a **PC/Camera** or
   **Source** button, set to camera). If it is set to its computer input,
   go to step 4 of [Fix](#fix).

## Fix

1. Select the document camera on the panel. **Expected:** the picture
   appears within a few seconds.
2. Press the camera's power button once, and wait about 10 seconds.
   **Expected:** its light comes on and the picture appears.
3. Unfold the arm, take off the lens cap and point the head at the desk.
   **Expected:** the desk appears on the screen.
4. Press the camera's **PC/Camera** or **Source** button until it shows the
   camera. **Expected:** the desk appears on the screen. If none of this
   gives a picture, escalate.

## Escalate

Escalate if the checks do not find the fault or the fix does not hold: to
AV engineering, through the [escalation matrix](../../procedures/index.md).
Say what the camera's lights showed and whether other sources worked.

## Close the ticket

Record the room, what you found, what you did, and this runbook's
identifier, so a repeat fault can be traced.
